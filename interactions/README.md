# Strategic Direction — Interaction Records

Dated, factual records of the direction-setting exchanges between EVEglyphDesign and the
Hawkins / Peterbilt Atlantic principals — Tim Hawkins, Luke Weatherbie, and Craig Allen.

This folder exists because the architecture in `architecture/` and the design in
`customer-sphere/` did not arrive from a specification document. It arrived from a
sequence of short, direct exchanges — messages, calls, working sessions — in which a
direction was stated, tested, and either adopted or set aside. When someone later asks
*why is the twin shaped this way*, the answer should be a dated record with the actual
words in it, not a reconstruction.

## What belongs here

- Direction stated to a principal, and the reply, where it changed or confirmed scope.
- The commitment or constraint that came out of the exchange.
- What it obligates the twin to build, with a link into the architecture that carries it.

## What does not belong here

- Private remarks, banter, character assessments, or third-party commentary. Records are
  redacted to the working content only. The paper trail is the design decision, not the
  conversation around it.
- Customer names, VINs, account numbers, phone numbers, or any dealership data. Records
  reference schema and design, never live records.
- Editorial commentary or grievance. That belongs in a document Dany owns directly.
