The four layers

1 · Systems of record

CDK, the legacy ASCII database and the PACCAR ePortal stay exactly as they are. The sidecar reads. It does not write, does not re-platform, and does not require a staff process to change.

2 · Extraction and landing

Metadata inventory first, then three full months into a Postgres mirror on the ASCII-safe schema — 21 objects, 443 confidence-tagged fields, generated DDL and a preflight validator. Financial reporting is keyed to the middle month.

3 · Analytics and decision support

Variance against 20-Group peer numbers rather than invented baselines. Parts availability reuses the dealer-parts-twin schema. Decision support must be able to say why an item ranks where it ranks.

4 · Output

Work lists ordered by urgency and strategy, delivered as reference material. The return path into CDK is Phase 2 and stays dashed until the gates clear.

Gates — real data does not move until

  • Tim's agreement is executed. Until then, synthetic dashboards and the mock composite stand in.
  • Luke has 20-Group access. Variance fields stay blank rather than guessed.
  • Call history ingests through a Peterbilt-owned TELUS service account, never personal credentials.
  • Signed user agreements and public access URLs exist for the systems in Craig Allen's 16-system register.
  • The public / protected repository split holds: client-facing material stays separate from the protected origin thesis.
