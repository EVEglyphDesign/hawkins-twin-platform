Phase 1 — Side Car Twin & Historical Archive

The entry point is a restored replica of the CDK backup set, not the live dealer management system. Hawkins' live system is never touched, queried, or written to. From that replica, three months of history are landed, modelled and scored to build the 20 Group performance surface: one dashboard, the structured data underneath it, and prioritised work lists delivered as reference material.

The five layers

1 · Systems of record

CDK dealer management, the legacy ASCII database, and the PACCAR ePortal carrying parts, warranty, logistics and production feeds. All read-only, all untouched.

2 · Backup replication

The CDK backup set is replicated into a restore we control. Repeatable and disposable. Zero production risk, and no dependency on a live integration window. A three month historical archive sits alongside it, with financial reporting keyed to the middle month so the boundary months cannot distort it.

3 · Landing and extraction

A Postgres mirror on the ASCII-safe schema already modelled at field level: 21 objects, 443 confidence-tagged fields, generated DDL and a preflight validator. From it, the 20 Group performance report is extracted as a defined metric set.

4 · Model and decision support

An Azure-hosted open-source model with pinned weights and a versioned prompt set, chosen so the output does not drift between runs and so cost and performance stay predictable. It drives variance against 20 Group peer numbers and an urgency by strategy ranking that can always explain why an item ranks where it ranks.

5 · The 20 Group performance surface

One dashboard, with the structured data underneath it, plus prioritised work lists delivered as reference material. Write-back into CDK stays out of scope: that is Phase 2 and it is gated.

Why the backup, and not the system

  • No production risk and no change to a running dealership.
  • No integration window to negotiate.
  • The replica is repeatable and disposable, so the build can be re-run from scratch.
  • Until Tim's agreement is executed, the same pipeline runs on synthetic and mock data without changing shape.

Gates — real data does not move until

  • Tim's agreement is executed. Until then, synthetic dashboards and the mock composite stand in.
  • Luke has 20-Group access. Variance fields stay blank rather than guessed.
  • Call history ingests through a Peterbilt-owned TELUS service account, never personal credentials.
  • Signed user agreements and public access URLs exist for the systems in Craig Allen's 16-system register.
  • The public / protected repository split holds: client-facing material stays separate from the protected origin thesis.
