Warranty GENE

Peterbilt Atlantic Digital Twin — the rich-target lane.

Gather. Eligibility. Notify. Earn.

The Warranty GENE is the module of the Hawkins/Peterbilt twin that answers one question per VIN, per day, ahead of the customer's next visit:

What warranty jobs should be performed on this truck, and how do we get it into a Hawkins bay before someone else does?

Warranty labor is the highest-margin work in Tim's shop. A job that costs the customer nothing can still return several thousand dollars of margin per event to the dealership. The Warranty GENE exists so no eligible truck ever leaves the region unclaimed.

What's inside

The six-rung signal ladder

From live SmartLINQ telemetry to the post-repair audit trail — ranked by how far ahead of the customer's next visit each source sees the truck.

The VIN-by-VIN diagnostic set

Identity, coverage window across ten buckets, open jobs by authority, three-number job scoring, and audit compliance flags.

PACCAR telemetry integration

How the twin turns PACCAR Solutions / SmartLINQ from an emergency system into a proactive warranty booking surface.

What it deliberately is not

Not a claim submission tool. Not a bypass of PACCAR authority. Not a data movement out of Hawkins.

Where it lands

  • Public surface — this page and the PDF at /warranty-gene/ under the Hawkins Twin Platform.
  • Private surface — rich-target worklists remain in Hawkins-owned Azure Postgres, indexed but never copied to the repository.
  • Executive surface — the warranty rich-target cohort becomes the fourth tile on the existing TELUS-style three-tile decision surface.
