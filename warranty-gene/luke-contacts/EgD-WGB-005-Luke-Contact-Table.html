Two rooftops, twenty-one counterparties, four columns per row: what to ask for, phone, email, portal or web form. Luke works down the list and sends every ask in the same week. Ask for: Not a vendor row. When a Torque Motorsports customer arrives with a BRP VIN whose administrator the sovereign replica does not already hold, the customer's paperwork (bill of sale, financing contract, VSC booklet) names the selling dealer.
