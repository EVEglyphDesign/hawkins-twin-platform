VIN coverage lookup — wireframe

Boxes and labels only. No data, no live reads. Purpose: agree the language and flow before code runs.
How to use this page. Pick one of the three modes below. Each mode walks you through the same underlying checks, in the order the operator needs to see them. Nothing is fetched. Nothing is stored. Every output box shows the shape of the answer, not a fake answer.

Pick a mode

Mode 1

Single VIN

Paste one VIN. Walk the five-step check. See the twin card at the end.

Mode 2

Work-order uplift

Upload a work-order file. See which line items could shift from customer-pay to warranty.

Mode 3

VIN batch scan

Upload or paste many VINs. Find the biggest warranty jobs across the whole cohort.

Step 1 of 5

Paste a VIN

A VIN is 17 characters. We validate the check digit locally in the browser. Nothing is sent anywhere yet.

What happens next. On Next, we decode the VIN against a public database and show the make, model, model year, and engine family. No dealer credentials involved at this step.

Step 1 of 4

Upload work orders

Excel or CSV. One row per line item. Minimum columns: RO, VIN, complaint or cause, customer-pay amount. Extra columns are preserved.

What happens next. We parse the file, group line items by VIN, and pull each VIN's coverage windows in the background.
Step 2 of 4

Parse and validate

Every VIN is check-digit-validated. Rows with a bad VIN, missing complaint, or missing dollar amount are flagged and held aside — nothing gets silently dropped.

Step 1 of 3

Upload or paste VINs

Excel, CSV, or paste-in — one VIN per line. Optional columns preserved: booking_date, rooftop, mileage, hours.

What happens next. Every VIN is validated, decoded, and coverage-checked. VINs with expired-or-expiring buckets sitting under known-common-failure components get flagged as big warranty jobs we should chase.
