§1The profile PACCAR is asking a dealer principal to fit

Every company has a profile. PACCAR's is measurable, scored, and re-tested every year. The evidence is in PACCAR's own press releases, its award citations, and its annual report to shareholders.

  • Excellence is scored, not asserted. Peterbilt's Best-in-Class dealer awards are decided on "Peterbilt Standard of Excellence scores, financial performance, parts and service performance and utilization of PACCAR programs and initiatives" — the fourth criterion means adopting PACCAR's programs is itself a measured dimension of dealer quality (Peterbilt, Feb 6 2026).
  • The top-of-network language is brand stewardship, not volume alone. Allstate Peterbilt was named 2025 North American Dealer Group of the Year for "top marks in Peterbilt's Standards of Excellence program," customer satisfaction, and "practices that strengthen the Peterbilt brand," per Peterbilt general manager and PACCAR vice president Jake Montero (Peterbilt, Feb 4 2026).
  • The visible proof points are certification counts. The same release quantifies Allstate as Platinum Service certified at 17 locations, Platinum Oval at 15, with 106 certified PACCAR MX technicians and 52 mobile service units (Peterbilt). The currency of standing is certified locations, certified techs, and mobile capacity.
  • Platinum Service Center status is a recurring test, not a badge. Certification is "granted... based on an objective set of criteria," with "scores assigned," and a dealer "must continue to meet or exceed the scoring threshold" (Peterbilt).
  • The Platinum criteria tell you exactly what PACCAR wants operationally. "World-class facilities and driver's lounges, utilization of PACCAR Solutions service management, expanded hours of service, excellent parts availability, certified MX engine technicians, lower dwell time, performing Rapid Check triage" — designated at the annual Peterbilt Dealer Meeting each February, with special designation in Peterbilt's dealer locator (Prestige Peterbilt certification page quoting then-GM Jason Skoog; Peterbilt Dealer Network).
  • On the Kenworth side, "World's Best Dealer" is 100% certification coverage plus captive-finance and TRP participation. Kenworth Sales Company won 2025 Dealer of the Year on heavy-duty share growth, 100% PremierCare Gold at all locations, four new stores, PACCAR Financial utilization, and TRP store participation, with Kenworth general manager and PACCAR vice president Jim Walenczak saying the dealer exemplifies "what it means to be a World's Best Dealer" (Kenworth, Feb 9 2026).
  • PremierCare Gold spells out the service model PACCAR expects. A 2-hour full diagnostic analysis and repair estimate via ExpressLane, dedicated ExpressLane technicians, mandatory extended evening and weekend hours, technicians at "the highest levels of certification under new and more rigorous factory certification standards for PACCAR MX series engines," MX parts stocking, 1-800-KW-ASSIST roadside, and TruckTech+ (Kenworth PremierCare Gold launch; PremierCare overview).
  • Parts performance is judged on loyalty share, not just sales. PACCAR Parts' 2025 Peterbilt dealer honours were awarded on year-over-year retail parts growth, purchase loyalty, TRP growth, and Fleet Services billings, per PACCAR Parts general manager and PACCAR vice president Bryan Sitko (PACCAR Parts, Feb 10 2026).
  • On the Kenworth parts side the 2025 award adds certification and fleet-services growth. CIT Trucks won for "excellence in parts business growth, customer satisfaction, and product knowledge," top PACCAR Parts growth, TRP volume and growth, Fleet Services increases, and "100% PremierCare Gold certification throughout the year" (PACCAR Parts, Feb 10 2026).
  • Dealer capital investment is publicly tallied as a measure of network quality. In 2025 Kenworth dealers "invested $218 million" and the network reached a record 498 locations including 351 PremierCare Gold Certified dealerships; Peterbilt's network "grew to 433 locations" with $195 million invested and 129 mobile service units added, for 1,143 total (PACCAR 2025 Annual Report).
  • Technician development is now an explicit dealer-principal expectation. Peterbilt's Master Technician designation — "the highest level of technician achievement" requiring "extensive hands-on experience, completion of rigorous coursework and demonstrated proficiency" — is framed by Danny Landholm, Peterbilt director of Dealer Network Development, as creating mentors who "ensure vehicles meet Peterbilt's high standards" (Peterbilt, Feb 23 2026). The Peterbilt Technician Institute is positioned as the pipeline "to meet the growing demand for skilled technicians across Peterbilt's dealer network" (Peterbilt, Jan 13 2026).
  • Captive-finance penetration is a stated PACCAR objective that runs through the dealer. "PFC financed 85 percent of dealers' new truck inventory and 21.1 percent of Kenworth and Peterbilt Class 8 trucks sold in the U.S. and Canada," with PFS retail share of 27.0% (2025 Annual Report; confirmed at 27% in the Q4 2025 earnings release).
  • Connected-truck adoption is the newest loyalty lever. "PACCAR Connect is installed in over 475,000 PACCAR vehicles" (2025 Annual Report), and SmartLINQ fault alerts are surfaced in the PACCAR Solutions portal and emailed to "your dealer," monitoring over 900 engine and emissions codes across four notification levels — Stop Now, Service Now, Service Soon, Informational (Peterbilt SmartLINQ sales sheet; Peterbilt Connected Services).
  • Growth math is set at the top and lands on dealers. PACCAR's stated ambition is 35% North American heavy-duty market share; 2025 combined Peterbilt + Kenworth share was 30.3% (Peterbilt 15.2%, Kenworth 15.1%) (Transport Topics, Kenworth GM transition; see also Transport Topics on PACCAR share).
  • Aftermarket and finance are where PACCAR's profit stability comes from. Dealer behaviour in those lines matters disproportionately. PACCAR Parts posted record $6.87B sales and $1.67B pre-tax in 2025, 24% of revenues, with parts sales expected up 4–8% in 2026 (2025 Annual Report); at the February 2026 Analyst Day parts and finance were characterised as roughly 71% of profits (MarketBeat, Feb 11 2026).
What this adds up to: The profile PACCAR is asking a dealer principal to fit is a certified, scored, high-utilization operator who leans into PACCAR programs — MX, Platinum/PremierCare Gold, PACCAR Solutions, PACCAR Financial, TRP, Fleet Services, PACCAR Connect — and who invests visibly in facilities and technicians. "Best" is measured, not claimed.

§7What PACCAR tells its owners

Direct quotations and figures from the PACCAR 2025 Annual Report and the January 27, 2026 Q4 2025 earnings release. Section headings are used in place of page numbers because the retrieved PDF text was section-tagged rather than paginated. Treat the annual-report link as a working copy of the document and re-verify the canonical path from PACCAR IR before quoting it in writing to PACCAR — 2025 Annual Report; Q4 2025 release.

Market share by region

  • "Peterbilt and Kenworth's Class 8 retail sales share in the U.S. and Canada was 30%. DAF's European 16+ tonne market share was 13.5%."
  • Medium duty 15.9%; PACCAR Mexico 36.4%; Australia a record 27.7%; DAF Brasil 8.6%; Colombia 18.0%.
  • MD&A – Truck: U.S./Canada heavy-duty share 29.9% in 2025 versus 30.7% in 2024.
  • Q4 2025 release: "Kenworth and Peterbilt achieved excellent U.S. and Canada Class 8 retail sales market share of 30% in 2025."
  • Longer-run ambition of 35% North American heavy-duty share, 2025 split of Peterbilt 15.2% / Kenworth 15.1% — Transport Topics; further Analyst Day tariff and manufacturing-flexibility commentary from SVP Laura Bloch at Transport Topics, Feb 13 2026.

Dealer-network quality

  • "The Kenworth dealer network grew to a record 498 locations, including 351 PremierCare Gold Certified dealerships. Dealers invested $218 million..."
  • Peterbilt's "network grew to 433 locations and invested $195 million," adding 129 mobile service units for 1,143 total.
  • Q4 2025 release: "PACCAR Parts' 21 global PDCs, with over 4 million square feet, support more than 2,000 DAF, Kenworth and Peterbilt sales, parts and service locations, and more than 350 TRP stores." CEO Preston Feight: "I am very proud of our employees and dealers who delivered outstanding trucks and transportation solutions to our customers."

Aftermarket parts strategy

  • Record sales of $6.87 billion and pre-tax income of $1.67 billion; 21 PDCs; "85% of parts within 24 hours"; TRP "expanded... to 365 locations in 49 countries"; the Managed Dealer Inventory (MDI) program; e-commerce up 15%; 2,900 fleets and 1.76 million vehicles supported; Parts equal 24% of revenues; 2026 parts sales expected up 4–8%.
  • Bryan Sitko in the Q4 2025 release: "PACCAR Parts provides strong foundational profitability through all phases of the business cycle. PACCAR Parts' excellent long-term growth reflects the benefits of investments in transportation solutions that increase customer loyalty and vehicle uptime. These investments include new parts distribution centers (PDCs), Fleet Services, and Managed Dealer Inventory programs."

Financial services

  • $485 million pre-tax income; retail finance market share 27.0%; "PFC financed 85 percent of dealers' new truck inventory and 21.1 percent of Kenworth and Peterbilt Class 8 trucks sold in the U.S. and Canada"; 26 countries; 226,000 units; $22.8 billion of assets.
  • Terren Drake in the Q4 2025 release: "PACCAR's strong balance sheet, complemented by its A+/A1 credit ratings, enables PFS to offer competitive retail financing to Kenworth, Peterbilt and DAF dealers and customers in 26 countries on four continents. PACCAR Financial Services has excellent access to the debt markets, issuing $3.12 billion in medium-term notes during 2025."

Connected truck and data strategy

  • "PACCAR Connect is installed in over 475,000 PACCAR vehicles," alongside investment in integrated truck data and agentic AI initiatives.

EV and alternative-powertrain roadmap

  • "Thirteen zero emissions models in production." A 30% stake in Amplify Cell Technologies and a 2.6-million-square-foot LFP battery cell plant at Byhalia, Mississippi. The 579EV rated up to 200 miles and the 567EV up to 250 miles; DAF XD/XF Electric named International Truck of the Year 2026; PACCAR Power Solutions delivered its 1,400th charger.
  • Timing risk publicly acknowledged elsewhere: hydrogen T680 FCEV serial production "will not begin in 2025 as previously announced" (Transport Topics) and Amplify cell production pushed to 2028 (Transport Topics).

ESG and safety commitments

  • CDP "A" score in 2025 and eleven consecutive years of A/A- ratings; top 13% in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment; ISO 14001 certification; more than 80% of facilities zero-waste-to-landfill; PACCAR Foundation giving of $260 million since 1951.
  • Safety content: Kenworth ADAS, ADAS PRO and ADAS PREMIER; Peterbilt Bendix Fusion and Electronic Park Brake.
  • The PACCAR ESG investor presentation and the SASB-ESG report name Scope 3 targets (25% Category 11 by 2030), EV charging, ZEV models, PACCAR Connect telematics, cybersecurity (NIST CSF), data privacy and DEI — but publicly they do not operationalize any of these down to dealers with a stated dealer requirement, target, disclosure duty or training program. That gap is where a dealer arriving with a sovereign, consent-first framework has room others do not.

Policy and capital context

  • Section 232 truck tariffs took effect in November 2025; 2026 capital spending guidance of $725–775 million and R&D of $450–500 million.
  • At the February 2026 Analyst Day, parts and financial services were characterised as approximately 71% of profits (MarketBeat).
