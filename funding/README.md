## Determination in one line

IRAP for what has not been built yet, SR&ED for what has already been built, ACOA for spreading it —
and none of it moves until a New Brunswick corporation is named as the claimant and a dated
experiment log is open.

## Blocking items

- **G1** No Canadian claimant entity designated. Both primary instruments require a Canadian claimant
  and Canadian-performed labour.
- **G2** IRAP cannot fund work already begun. The IRAP project must be scoped on the unresolved
  uncertainties (U3 telemetry-to-consumption linkage, U5 portability as a measured property), never on
  the completed parts twin.

enhanced SR&ED expenditure limit is now $6M, not $3M, and the claimant-entity question is resolved
in Section 07 of the extract.
