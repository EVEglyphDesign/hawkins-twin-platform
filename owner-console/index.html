The Owner Console

Conceptual Map — ATD Performance Measurement 2026 driving the Hawkins Twin

The 60 Critical Operating Variables published by ATD in the NADA Management Series Driven are the owner's ledger of truth. The Hawkins Twin translates those variables into a drill-downable console whose first question is not how are we doing but where is the next dollar — which customers to accelerate, which vendors to press, which trucks to move.
